NRD Exporter

Export Exempt Market Dealer firms, their Dealing Representatives and any terms & conditions from the CSA National Registration Search to Excel.

1. Install

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar. If the bar is hidden, press ⌘⇧B (Chrome, Edge, Safari).

You only do this once. The bookmark loads the latest version of the tool from GitHub every time you click it, so updates arrive on their own.

NRD Exporter

No bookmarks bar? Open the NRD site, open DevTools (⌥⌘J), paste the script into the Console and press Enter.

2. Run

  1. Open National Registration Search.
  2. Click the NRD Exporter bookmark. A panel opens at the top right.
  3. Leave the category as Exempt Market Dealer. Pick provinces if you only want some, or none for all.
  4. For a quick test, set Max firms to 5 and click Start new run. Set it back to 0 for a full run.
  5. Click Download Excel (all tabs). It works mid-run too, with the data collected so far. Single-tab CSVs are there as well.

How long it takes. The NRD site allows about one request per second, and the tool stays at that pace. For all ~810 EMD firms: the firm list and firm details take about 15 minutes. Listing the people at each firm takes about another hour. Checking each person's categories, which is what identifies Dealing Representatives, is one page per person and takes several more hours. To go faster, tick only the provinces you need, or untick Individual categories if the full list of registered people is enough.

Stopping and resuming. Progress is saved in this browser as it goes. Click Stop, reload, or close the tab whenever you like. Later, open the NRD site, click the bookmark, and choose Resume. The tab can stay in the background while it runs.

What you get: one Excel workbook

FirmsEverything on the NRD firm record: NRD #, name, other and previous names, EMD provinces and earliest EMD date, all provinces, other categories, head office (street, city, province, postal code, country), phone, Disciplined List flag with SEDAR+ link, no-longer-registered flag, firm terms & conditions, counts of registered people and EMD dealing reps, NRD link
Firm RegistrationsOne row per firm, province and category, with the start date. Easy to filter, for example "EMD in Ontario since 2024".
IndividualsEveryone registered at those firms: EMD dealing rep yes/no, which provinces and since when, other dealing rep registrations, officer and manager roles (CCO, UDP, branch manager), terms & conditions, Disciplined List with SEDAR+ link, other firms, NRD link
Dealing RepsSame columns, filtered to Dealing Representative (Exempt Market Dealer)
T&C SummaryCounts by condition category and province, for firms, dealing reps and all registrants
Firms with T&COne row per firm per condition category, with the provinces it applies in and the wording
People with T&COne row per person per condition category: dealing rep yes/no, firm, provinces, supervision level, wording
Terms & ConditionsEvery condition, one row per province, with its categories, the NRD's own label, supervision level and full wording
AboutWhen and how the file was made, and whether the run was complete

Every sheet has a frozen, filterable header row, real Excel dates, and clickable NRD links.

Terms & conditions scan and report

Click Scan for terms & conditions at the top of the panel. It reads every EMD's record and everyone registered at them. Tick provinces first to narrow it. Then click Open T&C report for a page you can filter by:

  • Who: firms, dealing reps, or all registrants
  • Province
  • Category

The report shows a category-by-province table (click any number to drill in) and the firms or people grouped under each category, with the full wording and NRD links. Save T&C report keeps a copy as a file.

Categories in plain words (strict or close supervision, restricted clients, restricted products, conflicts, compliance monitor, capital and financial reporting, non-resident firm and so on) are worked out from the wording of each condition. One condition can fall under several. The NRD's own label is always shown beside it.

Notes

  • It runs in your browser tab and uses the same data calls as the NRD pages, one at a time. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
  • If the site says to slow down (HTTP 429), the tool pauses, lowers its pace, and continues. Lookups that still fail are listed in the log, and Resume retries them.
  • The site shows a reCAPTCHA after about 15 searches in a session. A run uses one search. If the CAPTCHA appears, the panel tells you to tick it on the search page and start again.
  • The NRD site blocks non-browser tools (scripts, servers). That's why this runs as a bookmarklet.
  • Data is only as current as the NRD refresh date shown on the site.
